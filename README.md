# Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive sales dashboard built in Excel using PivotTables, PivotCharts, Power Pivot, and slicers to explore order, revenue, and delivery data.

Screenshot 2026-09-14 125529.png

## Overview

- **Total Orders:** 1,000
- **Total Revenue:** ₹586,176.00
- **Avg. Days Between Order and Delivery:** 5.72
- **Avg. Customer Spending:** ₹4,652.19

## What's in the dashboard

- **Revenue by Occasion** — revenue broken down across Anniversary, Birthday, Diwali, Holi, Raksha Bandhan, and Valentine's Day
- **Revenue by Category** — Colors, Mugs, Raksha Bandhan, Soft Toys, Sweets
- **Revenue by Hour (Order Time)** — order revenue trend across a 24-hour cycle
- **Revenue by Months** — monthly revenue trend across the year
- **Top 5 Products by Revenue**
- **Top 10 Cities by Orders**
- Interactive slicers for **Order Date**, **Delivery Date**, and **Occasion**

## Tools used

- Excel PivotTables & PivotCharts
- Power Pivot (data model)
- Slicers for interactive filtering

## Key insights

- Anniversary is the top-performing occasion by revenue, while Diwali brings in the least among the tracked occasions.
- Soft Toys is by far the leading product category, generating roughly 3x the revenue of the next-closest category.
- Order activity shows clear peaks around early morning and early evening hours, suggesting distinct customer ordering windows worth investigating further.
- Kavali leads all cities in order volume among the top 10 tracked.

## Data Source
- Practice dataset (Ferns & Petals sales data) sourced from a GitHub repo linked in a YouTube tutorial's description. Used for learning/portfolio purposes.
